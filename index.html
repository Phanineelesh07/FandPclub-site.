Film & Photography Club

Aditya University

Capture Moments. Create Stories.

The Film & Photography Club of Aditya University is a creative platform for students passionate about visual storytelling.

The club focuses on photography, filmmaking, editing, and cinematography through workshops, photo walks, and short film projects.

It helps students enhance their creativity, technical skills, and storytelling abilities while providing opportunities to showcase their work in competitions and events.

THE 24 CRAFTS OF CINEMA

From the first word of the script to the final color grade, our club unites students passionate about every single craft of filmmaking.

DIRECTOR

The visionary guiding the story, actors, and overall creative direction.

CINEMATOGRAPHER

Painting with light and lenses to capture the perfect cinematic shot.

WRITER

Architects of the narrative, building characters and worlds from scratch.

EDITOR

Sculpting time and rhythm, transforming raw footage into visual experiences.

MUSIC DIRECTOR

Creating the auditory world through Music,Foley, mixing, and soundscapes.

+ 19 OTHER CRAFTS
